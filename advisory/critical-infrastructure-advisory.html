Critical Infrastructure Operations

Resilience • Data Center Operations • Power & Cooling • Governance & Compliance

Overview

Critical infrastructure operations depend on disciplined routines, clear ownership, reliable documentation and practical decision-making under pressure.

This note outlines key operational focus areas that influence reliability, resilience and long-term infrastructure maturity in data centers and other mission-critical environments.
