About

What I do, and where I learned it.

I started in IT management in 1998, and was head of IT for the City of Oslo's planning and building services until 2011.

In 2011 I moved into data centers. From 2016 to 2024 I was Critical Facility Manager at OS‑IX in Oslo, responsible for power, cooling, DCIM, security, staff and vendors.

Today I work as a Technical Account Manager at a data center.

Here you will find the principles I work by, and examples of how I structure procedures and documentation.

Operating principles

The principles I work by.

01

Reliability before complexity.

A solution should make operations stronger, not add more things that can break.

02

Clear ownership.

Every system, decision and follow-up needs a name next to it.

03

Stay calm under pressure.

Calm comes from preparation, clear roles and short, clear messages.

04

Documentation that gets used.

A procedure is only good if it is current and easy to follow at 3 a.m.

05

Keep it maintainable.

The best solution is the one your team can run safely, year after year.
