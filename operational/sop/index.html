Operational Library / SOP

Standard Operating Procedure Examples

Examples for routine work: inspections, walk-rounds and access. Work done the same way every time, under normal conditions.

Purpose

Routine work done the same way by everyone, without relying on memory.

What an SOP is for

An SOP covers routine work under normal conditions. The same steps every time, whoever is on shift.

If the work removes redundancy or changes a system, it needs a MOP. If something has already gone wrong, it is an EOP.

Examples

SOP-GEN-002

Generator Weekly Inspection

The weekly walk-round that catches the simple things that stop a generator from starting.

SOP-SEC-003

Data Hall Access Control

Who gets in, to do what, and who watches. Most of the risk comes from people who are allowed in.

SOP-CLG-004

Cooling System Inspection

The routine walk-round that finds a standby unit that isn’t ready, a growing hot spot or a drain that has started to leak.

How the examples are built

The SOPs here follow the same pattern: why the work matters, what to check before you start, who does what, the steps, what to do if something is wrong, and what to check afterwards.

The blank SOP template (Word) uses the same pattern.
