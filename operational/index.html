Operational Library

Operational Library

Examples of the documents I would want on a data center site: procedures for normal work, planned work and emergencies, and the structure that keeps them usable.

1. About these examples

I wrote every example here for this site, from experience, public standards and common practice. None of them comes from a live site. Before anything here is used for real, it needs local review, a technical check and approval by the people responsible for that site.

2. What's in the library

MOP

EOP

3. How the procedures are built

Every procedure in the library follows the same pattern:

  • People and safety come first. Declaring an incident is never step one.
  • Every staffing level is covered: no one on site, one person, two or more, and security.
  • The real risk is stated plainly, with what to do if the worst case happens during the work.
  • Hold points mark where the work stops until someone confirms.
  • There is always a planned way back to normal.

Every example uses the same neutral asset tags, such as SITE1-B1-ER2-UPS-001-01. How they are built: see Asset Identification.
