Operational Library / Templates

Operational Documentation Templates

Blank Word templates for SOPs, MOPs and EOPs, and an Excel file for risk assessment. They follow the same structure as the examples in this library.

Purpose

A starting point for your own procedures. Each one still needs local review and approval.

Downloads

SOP

SOP template

For routine work. Steps with tag, check, owner and sign-off, and what to do if you find something wrong.

MOP

MOP template

For planned work and changes. Change type, risk assessment reference, hold points, rollback and the rule for restarting stopped work.

EOP

EOP template

For emergencies. Activation, people first, timed first steps, escalation and the way back to normal.

RISK

Risk assessment template

Excel. Likelihood and consequence from 1 to 5, with consequence for people, service, equipment and cost. Scores before and after controls, stop conditions, and who accepts each level. Three worked examples.

The three Word templates have the control fields from the Governance page: ID, revision, status, owner, approval, next review date, classification and a revision log. Every page is marked “Uncontrolled when printed”.

How to use them

  • Replace the grey text with your own, then delete it.
  • Name assets by their tags. See Asset Identification.
  • Only write steps that people are trained and allowed to do.
  • Put a hold point before anything that removes redundancy or can drop load.
  • Have someone who knows the system review it before approval.

A template is only a starting point. The finished procedure is what gets approved.
